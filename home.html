About the Parish

A Fulfilled Transformation

by Malou Lauzon-Manrique

A place of worship, solace and a sanctuary — this is where a parishioner or devotee yearns when one visits a Church.

Did you know that our beloved parish church had two Dedication Rites? The first one was on January 10, 1982, when the Archbishop of Manila, Jaime Cardinal L. Sin, took note of the letter “L” shape of the church, stressing that it stands for the word “Love.”

“The letter ‘L’ shape of the church stands for the word ‘Love.’”

The second Dedication Rites happened on February 20, 2016, after construction from ground zero in August 2012. Parishioners never faltered in pursuit of all sorts of fundraisers — from “Dream Fulfilled 888” at the time of Rev. Fr. Arthur “Aytoy” T. Opiniano, and in 2012, “fb8” during the time of Rev. Fr. Herbel “Dodot” F. Torres.

The Fatima Parish Church is not just an edifice but a nurturing, grace-filled and all-embracing structure for devotees and parishioners from all walks of life.

Our story

Over the Years

Five decades of faith, built by the shepherds who served here and the parishioners who never faltered.

  1. 1974

    The Parish Is Established

    Our Lady of Fatima Parish begins, with Msgr. Agerico L. Galang as its first parish priest.

  2. 1982

    First Dedication Rites

    On January 10, Jaime Cardinal L. Sin dedicates the newly finished three-storey church — its “L” shape standing for “Love.”

  3. 2012

    Rebuilding from Ground Zero

    Construction of the new church begins in August, carried by parish fundraisers “Dream Fulfilled 888” and “fb8.”

  4. 2016

    Second Dedication Rites

    The new church is dedicated on February 20, 2016.

  5. 2018

    The Carillon Bells

    Four carillon bells are installed on the belfry during the tenure of Rev. Fr. Henry Ferreras.

  6. 2020

    Steadfast Amidst the Pandemic

    With no public Masses for more than a year, Msgr. Dan Sta. Maria continues to celebrate daily Mass in the convent prayer room, and Sunday Masses are streamed online.

  7. 2021

    Community Pantries & a New Shepherd

    The first OLFP community pantry is held on April 25 — six in all that year, serving almost 500 beneficiaries. Rev. Fr. Fredrick Edward “Jojo” C. Simon becomes parish priest.

  8. Today

    The Adoration Chapel

    A gift of faith from Ms. Monica Aveo, the Adoration Chapel beside the church entrance lets parishioners keep vigil throughout the day.

Sacrament of Baptism

Baptism Requirements & Guidelines

Through Baptism we are freed from sin, reborn as children of God, and welcomed into the Church.

Requirements

  • Birth Certificate of the child (photocopy with original CTC or PSA)
  • Marriage Contract, if married (photocopy with original CTC or PSA)
  • Letter of Permission from the Parish Priest, if the candidate is not from this parish
  • Application Form to be filled up by the father or mother of the child c/o OLFP
  • Proof of identity of parents / ID
  • Certificate of No Baptismal Record from 3 parishes near your residence (for 2 years old and above)
  • Seminar/Catechism every Saturday, 4:00 PM, prior to your scheduled date — No Seminar, No Baptism

Fees

Includes 1 pair of sponsors; additional sponsors ₱50.00 per person

Solo Baptism

By appointment, at least a week before your desired date

Includes 1 pair of sponsors; additional sponsors ₱100.00 per person

₱3,000.00

Rules & Regulations

  1. Bookings/reservations are to be made at least 2 weeks before your desired date.
  2. Reservation fee is non-refundable and non-transferable.
  3. Cancellation means forfeiture of payment.
  4. Parents and at least 1 pair of sponsors must attend the Catechism/Seminar prior to the scheduled date of Baptism (every Saturday at 4:00 PM).
  5. Strictly white dress and cap for the child.
  6. Sponsors must be baptized and confirmed Catholics, 18 years old and above.
  7. Parents, sponsors and guests must wear proper attire inside the Church (no sleeveless, tube, mini skirts or shorts).
  8. If you invite your own priest, please notify us in writing with his full name, title/designation, and a photocopy of his Celebret ID.
  9. Punctuality is a must — other church/priest services may be scheduled.
  10. The baptismal certificate will be available one (1) month after the baptism date. The first copy contains the complete list of sponsors and is free of charge if claimed within one (1) year from the date of baptism; succeeding copies will contain only the names of the principal sponsors and will be charged accordingly.

Sacrament of Matrimony

Weddings at Our Lady of Fatima Parish

General information for couples planning to be married at Our Lady of Fatima Parish. Bookings and reservations must be made at least three (3) months before the scheduled date, and all requirements must be completed at least two (2) weeks before the wedding.

Very important: please read everything below. All documents must be ORIGINAL.

A Basic Requirements (all couples)

  1. Newly issued (within 6 months) Baptismal Certificates of the groom and bride from the church where they were baptized, with the notation: For Marriage Purposes.
  2. Confirmation Certificates of the groom and bride from the church where they received the Sacrament of Confirmation, with the notation: For Marriage Purposes.
  3. Marriage License issued by the Civil Registrar of the city or town of either the bride or groom.
  4. If the couple has been civilly married: a copy of the Marriage Contract from the Philippine Statistics Authority.
  5. If the couple has been living-in for five years or more: 4 copies of an Affidavit of Cohabitation.
  6. Birth Certificates of the groom and bride issued by the Philippine Statistics Authority.
  7. Single Status Certificates (Civil Status Certificate of No Marriage / CENOMAR) of the groom and bride issued by the Philippine Statistics Authority.
  8. Four (4) copies of a newly taken (within 6 months) 2×2 inch ID photo of the groom and bride.
  9. Canonical Interview of the groom and bride, conducted at least 1 month before the wedding date.
  10. Ecclesiastical Banns — announcement/publication of the marriage in the parishes of the groom and bride. The forms will be provided by this parish; please bring them to your respective parishes and return them after publication.
  11. Permit from the parish of the bride, if she does not belong to this parish. The appropriate form will be provided by our parish office.
  12. Pre-Cana Seminar to be attended by the groom and bride.
  13. A list of the sponsors with their complete addresses, submitted in typewritten form. (Note: 6 pairs only.)
  14. It is highly recommended that the groom and bride receive the Sacrament of Penance (confession) for a fruitful reception of the Sacrament of Marriage.

Additional Requirements

Open the case that applies to you.

B Previous Marriage
  1. A Certified True Copy of the Marriage Contract, duly annotated (of court decision), from the Philippine Statistics Authority.
  2. A widower or widow must present the Death Certificate of his/her deceased partner from the Philippine Statistics Authority.
  3. Those whose marriage has been annulled must present the Document of Nullity from the competent Matrimonial Tribunal, and the Court Annulment Decision with Certificate of Finality, with a letter of approval from the Chancery Office of the Diocese of Cubao (41 Lantana St., Cubao, Quezon City).
  4. Those whose civil marriage has been annulled must present the Court Annulment Decision with Certificate of Finality.
  5. A divorcee must present a Certified True Copy of the Decree of Divorce from the County Court Recorder.
C Marriage with a Foreigner
  1. Photocopy of Passport.
  2. Search of Marriage (Proof of Singleness) from the State or Country Recorder / Registry of Marriages abroad.
  3. Photocopy of the ACR/ICR for resident foreigners in the Philippines.
  4. Newly issued “Certification of Freedom to Marry” from his/her present parish priest abroad, certifying that he/she has no trace of marriage found in the parish registry and no canonical impediment to contract marriage in the Roman Catholic Church.
  5. A Certificate of Legal Capacity to Marry or Embassy Clearance from the foreigner’s consulate/embassy.
  6. A Letter of Clearance from the Chancery Office of the Diocese of Cubao (41 Lantana St., Cubao, Quezon City).
D Catholic & Non-Catholic
  1. Newly issued certificate of baptism or dedication, or certification of being a bona fide member of a non-Catholic religion.
  2. A newly issued “Certification of No Impediment” from his/her present pastor, certifying that he/she is free from any marriage impediment and is allowed to get married in the Roman Catholic Church.
  3. Additional canonical forms for the application of marriage clearance:
    • I. Formula for mixed marriage (disparity of worship/cult) — declaration of promise by the Catholic party
    • II. Request letter for Mass (from the couple)
    • III. Endorsement letter from the Parish Priest
    • IV. Result of Canonical Interview/Interrogation
    • V. Marriage Expectation Inventory
    • VI. Affidavit of the applicant’s freedom to marry — must be executed before a priest-interviewer together with two witnesses: (a) an immediate family member and (b) a friend of at least five years (bride & groom)
  4. Publication of Banns for the Catholic party.
  5. For No Religion / Iglesia Ni Cristo / Muslim: a notarized “Affidavit of Affirmation for the Religion and Singleness.”
  6. Dispensation for mixed religion or disparitas cultus from the Chancery Office of the Diocese of Cubao (41 Lantana St., Cubao, Quezon City).
E Immigrants / Permanent Residents Abroad
  1. Photocopy of the Permanent Resident ID for an immigrant / permanent resident abroad.
  2. Photocopy of Proof of Residency abroad.
  3. Photocopy of Passport.
  4. Search of Marriage (Proof of Singleness) from the State or Country Recorder / Registry of Marriages abroad.
  5. Newly issued “Certification of Freedom to Marry” from his/her present parish priest abroad, certifying that he/she has no trace of marriage found in the parish registry and no canonical impediment to contract marriage in the Roman Catholic Church.
  6. A Letter of Clearance from the Chancery Office of the Diocese of Cubao (41 Lantana St., Cubao, Quezon City).
F Seafarers
  1. Photocopy of Passport, Seaman’s Book and Company ID.
  2. Newly issued “Certification of Affirmation of No Claimed Marriage” from the ship captain or company, certifying that there is no known marriage and no impediment to contract marriage.

G Parish Regulations

  1. Bookings and reservations are to be made at least three (3) months before the scheduled date to give ample time for preparations and processing of documents. Please inform us in writing of any changes in date or time as early as possible through e-mail or fax.
  2. A non-refundable ₱5,000.00 down payment is made and the contract signed for reservation.
  3. We charge a total of ₱18,000.00 for the wedding (price is subject to change without prior notice). This includes use of the church, candles, servers, church coordinator, organist and soloist during the Mass (pictorial not included), flowers (aisle only), stipend for the officiating priest, and processing of documents. The balance must be paid one (1) month before the wedding.
  4. All requirements must be completed at least two (2) weeks before the date of the wedding. We reserve the right to cancel a wedding if the requirements are not complete.
  5. If you get your own priest, please advise us a month before, in writing, of his full name, title or designation, the number and expiry date of his Authority to Solemnize Marriages (with photocopy), a photocopy of his Faculty from the Diocese of Cubao, and his Celebret ID.
  6. If you prepare your own missalette, please follow the official rites from the parish.
  7. Please observe basic rules of decency in dress for the entire entourage.
  8. Throwing of confetti or rice and use of a fog machine are not allowed.
  9. Only liturgical songs are to be sung during the Mass. Love songs and other secular songs may be sung after the rites (during the contract signing, picture taking, etc.).
  10. Punctuality is a must — the wedding ceremony should start as scheduled. Please be aware that there may be other church services scheduled.

The couple must provide the following:

  • Offerings: one pair of candles in glass, bread and wine (host and Mass wine), and any kind offering such as fruits, goods, etc.
  • Wedding rings, arrhae, Bible (optional), veil, cord, and match or lighter.
