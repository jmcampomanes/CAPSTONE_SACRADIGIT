Seed Mock Certificate Requests

Dev-only utility. Writes the certificate requests of the people in fake-account-candidates.csv (511 requests by 388 people) into Record Requests, together with the register entry each one points to in Digital Archives. Each request is already linked to its record, so a parishioner only has to verify it.

Archive documents: uploads the 10 renamed Villaruel scans (marriage, civil marriage, 4 confirmations, 4 birth certificates) and adds each to Digital Archives. Uploaded files stay in storage after Clear Mock Data, like any archive delete.

Rebuild the data: node tools/mock-community/seed-csv-requests.mjs path/to/fake-account-candidates.csv

Cleanup: "Clear Mock Data" deletes exactly the rows this tool created (tracked in this browser).

Note: the requests are owned by whoever runs this page. To see them under My Requests, sign in as the matching account.
