Decide: who approves what?

The Secretary cannot approve certificate requests (those buttons are Head-Admin-only), but can approve and decline Blessings, Facility Bookings and Mass Intentions; nothing on those pages checks the role. That may be on purpose (the office handles bookings day to day), but it's an uneven rule. If it's intended, say so on the page ("You can approve blessings and bookings; certificates go to the Head Admin"). If not, hide those buttons the same way.
Where: blessings.js, facility-booking.js, mass-intentions.js have no isHeadAdmin() / head-admin-only checks.

B. Find a parishioner from anywhereBoth

Walk-in questions usually start with a name: "Did my baptismal certificate come through? Is my house blessing still on?" A search box in the top bar (or Ctrl+K) could find that name across requests, blessings, bookings, intentions and records, and show it as one timeline. That saves opening four pages while the person waits at the counter.
