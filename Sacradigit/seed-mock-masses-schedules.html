Seed Mock Masses & Special Schedules

Dev-only utility. Creates a few weeks of realistic Mass records (weekday, Saturday anticipated, Sunday, plus a handful of special/baptism masses) spread across today and the next month and a half, and — where the backend supports it — a few Special Schedules with overlapping date ranges, so the Calendar View on both pages has something real to show. Every seeded record is tagged so it can be cleanly removed afterward.

Heads up: the SpecialSchedule model isn't deployed to this backend yet (same limitation the Special Schedules page already flags), so this tool will seed Masses only and skip Special Schedules until that model is added.

Masses: weekday Daily Masses (6/7 AM), a Saturday Anticipated Mass, four Sunday Masses, plus a First Friday devotion, two Baptism Masses, and a few named feast-day masses — spread from about a week ago through six weeks from today so you can page the calendar across three different months.

Special Schedules (when connected): an ongoing Liturgical Season spanning the whole window, plus a Novena, a Feast Day Series, and a Special Event with overlapping date ranges, so you can see the "marker on every day in range" calendar behavior and multiple schedules active on the same day.

Cleanup: "Clear Mock Data" deletes exactly the records this tool created (tracked in this browser), not anything you've entered yourself.
