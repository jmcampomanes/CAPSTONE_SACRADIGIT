Roles & Access

Accounts

Everyone who can sign in. A person’s role decides which portal they can open: Head Admin and Secretary → Admin portal (a Secretary can’t change schedules, approve certificate requests, or publish announcements), IT Team → Sacra ITech, Media Team → Media portal. Everyone can use the Parishioner portal. Parishioners create their own accounts; add staff here.

Account management isn’t set up on the backend yet. Deploy the sign-in update (listUsers, setUserRole, createStaffUser, setUserEnabled) to manage accounts here.
