Parishioner portal on a phone. Problems found by testing, then suggestions. Tell me which letters/numbers to build.

Shorter My Badges B. Ministry: remember + Mine C. Shorter intro boxes D. "Your next thing" card E. Cancel / reschedule on the card F. Text or email reminders
How this was tested: all 16 parishioner pages opened in Chrome on a 390px phone screen, plus the open ☰ menu, and the Dashboard and certificate form again at 360px with Bigger text on. A simulated signed-in parishioner ("Rosa") was given sample records; nothing reached the live system. Each page was also measured for sideways scrolling, text under 12px and tap targets under 32px.
Already working: no page scrolls sideways; the labeled top bar, solid navy buttons, Dashboard request cards, renamed menu with "More below", "What do you need?" on Request a Service, the plain-sentence certificate status and full-width Volunteer buttons all show correctly.
Companion pages: first parishioner mockups · guideline review.
